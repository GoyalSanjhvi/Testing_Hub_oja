How do we cope with a miscarriage after a long fertility journey?

11:56 AM

I'm sorry you're going through this. Oja's content speaks directly to this: **The emotional reality:** Miscarriage is a genuine loss, and feelings of grief, anger, and sadness are completely valid—not overreactions. This is especially true after a long fertility journey, where the emotional weight has already been heavy. **What can help:** - Support from your partner, from others who have experienced loss, and from counselling - There should be no shame in seeking professional support—compassion, both from others and toward yourself, is part of recovery - If you're in serious distress, reach out to a qualified professional or trusted person Oja also notes that if you've experienced repeated losses, couples are encouraged to seek specialist assessment rather than continuing to try without support. A doctor can help identify whether targeted treatment might improve outcomes. **Immediate support:** The Tele-MANAS helpline (14416) is available for emotional support. This is a real loss deserving real care. Please don't carry this alone.

Does PCOS get worse with age?

11:56 AM

Oja's content doesn't cover how PCOS progresses or changes with age. What it does explain is that PCOS is one of the most common hormonal conditions in women of reproductive age, often marked by irregular ovulation, signs of higher male-type hormones (like acne or excess hair), and a characteristic ovarian appearance on ultrasound. Many cases involve insulin resistance, which links PCOS to weight gain and a higher long-term risk of type 2 diabetes. For specific questions about how your PCOS may change over time or what to expect as you age, a qualified healthcare provider who knows your individual situation would be the best source.
